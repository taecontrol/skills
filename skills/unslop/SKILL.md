# Unslop

Use as a final edit for prose written for people. It does not replace technical review or decide content. Preserve the governing style and required structure.

## Edit pass

1. Identify the audience, purpose, target language, and required format.
2. Preserve facts, uncertainty, sourced claims, code, commands, identifiers, domain terms, quotations, legal wording, and required syntax.
3. Cut filler, puffery, chatbot language, vague attribution, repeated framing, generic conclusions, and unsupported claims. Replace vague claims only with supported specifics.
4. Use direct verbs, plain words, stable terminology, and clear sentences. Keep passive voice when the actor is unknown or irrelevant.
5. Remove unnecessary jargon and decorative formatting. Retain punctuation or form required by the language, quotation, style, or technical content.
6. Read the result in its target language. Preserve natural phrasing and punctuation, especially in Spanish.
7. Confirm that meaning, qualifiers, technical distinctions, and evidence remain unchanged.

## Completion criteria

- Every material detail and required format remains faithful.
- The prose is concrete, direct, and natural in its target language.
- The edit adds no evidence, changes no claim, and weakens no necessary qualifier.
