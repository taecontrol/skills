# Answer

The task produces an answer: how something works, why it is so, what state it is in, or which option to take. Nothing changes.

- **Owners.** `how` owns how a system works, `why` how it came to be, `research` a question that sources outside the project answer, and `spike` one that only an experiment settles.
- **When to stop looking.** An experiment earns its place when its result would change what the human decides. An open risk is named in the answer, and chased only when they ask.
- **Real resources.** An experiment is a check: step 1 says when it needs the human's word. Remove what it created.
- **The report is the answer,** in its first line, then its sources and what you could not establish. It is usually the only stop. When the answer points to work, propose it in one line: starting it is a change of mode, and theirs to decide. An answer written where other people read it is shown to the human first.
