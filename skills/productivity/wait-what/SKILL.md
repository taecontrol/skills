Wait, I don't understand where you've got to here. Re-pitch that with enough context for the last message to stand on its own.

Use the user's active language, English or Spanish, unless they request the other. In English, talk in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English. In Spanish, use plain language guided by UNE-ISO 24495-1:2024: lead with the key point, use familiar words and short direct sentences, and define essential technical terms. Write natural Spanish rather than translated English idioms.
