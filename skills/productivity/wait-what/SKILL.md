description: "Re-pitch a message so it stands on its own. Use when the user does not follow your last message or one item of it (\"no entiendo\", \"I don't follow\", \"¿qué es X?\", \"what do you mean?\", \"Q3. No entiendo\"), asks again what to decide or do, or asks for their language after you wrote in another; and before you pass another agent's report on to the user."
---

Re-pitch the message so it stands on its own for someone who has not seen the work behind it: your last message when it did not land, or another agent's report before you pass it on.

Lead with what the user must decide or do, or with the answer they asked for. Then add only the context the lead needs to make sense. Replace or define in a few words every ID, label, and term the user has not used themselves, such as an example ID, a ticket code, or a type name.

Use the user's active language, English or Spanish, unless they request the other. In English, talk in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English. In Spanish, use plain language guided by UNE-ISO 24495-1:2024: lead with the key point, use familiar words and short direct sentences, and define essential technical terms. Write natural Spanish rather than translated English idioms, and keep the user's register, tú or usted.
