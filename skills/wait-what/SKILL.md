# Wait, what?

Use only when the user asks for a re-explanation or says the previous message was not understood. Pause the affected communication or decision thread.

## Re-explain

1. Use the user's active language, Spanish or English, unless they request the other.
2. Open with what was being decided or reported and the current boundary.
3. Restate the message in short, plain sentences. Define necessary technical terms before using them.
4. Give a concrete example when the point would otherwise remain abstract. Keep facts, decisions, recommendations, and planned actions distinct.
5. Stop at the original boundary and wait for the user before resuming the paused thread.

Keep exact names, commands, identifiers, quotations, and material legal or technical wording unchanged. Write natural Spanish, not translated English idioms.

## Completion criteria

- The user triggered the recovery.
- The explanation uses the requested or active language and identifies its context.
- Necessary terms are clear, and abstract points have an example.
- It introduces no decision or progress beyond the original boundary.
