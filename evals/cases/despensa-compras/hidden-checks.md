# Hidden checks

You check one build of Despensa against a fixed list. The worktree path is named at the end of this brief. Despensa here has no screens for these features: you drive it over HTTP, and over MCP where a check says so.

- **Work in your own copy.** Others use the same worktree at the same time, and the product keeps its database inside the checkout. Make a copy with `git -C <worktree> worktree add --detach <a new directory outside it> HEAD`, copy the worktree's `.work/` into it, work there, and remove it with `git worktree remove --force` when you finish.
- **Launch** the product in the copy: `vp install`, then apply the build's migrations to the copy's local D1 (`vp exec wrangler d1 migrations apply DB --local`), then `vp dev --port <a free port>`. **Start every check from an empty database**, unless it says otherwise: stop the server, delete the copy's `.wrangler/state`, apply the migrations again, and restart. Seed what the check needs yourself.
- **Call operations** with `POST http://localhost:<port>/api/<operation>` and a JSON body. A declared failure answers with a non-2xx status and a `_tag` that names it, at the top of the body or under `error`. Judge by the outcome and the `_tag`, not by the status code or the message wording.
- **Data:** `.work/specs/receipts.json` holds the Coto and Farmcity receipts as `record_purchase` inputs without a `requestId`. Add a fresh `requestId` to each call unless a check says to reuse one. "Coto" and "Farmcity" below mean those receipts. Every line you build yourself needs a `newProduct` with a name, unless the check gives it a `productId`.
- **Concurrent calls** are sent at the same moment, for example with `Promise.all` in a Node script or `xargs -P`.
- **Judge each check by its expected result alone.** Names of fields beyond the contract, message wording and ordering of keys may differ from build to build.

Answer with one JSON object:

```json
{ "checks": [ { "id": "H1", "result": "pass|fail|blocked", "evidence": "the calls you sent and the answers you got, abridged" } ] }
```

## Phase 1: recording, correcting, deleting and finding purchases

Run these after phase 1, and again after phase 2.

- **H1, Exact sums of huge amounts.** Send a Farmcity receipt without `receipt`, with two lines without codes, `LARGE` with amount `90071992547409.9` and `SMALL` with amount `0.03`, a receipt-wide adjustment of `-90071992547409.9`, and total `0.02`. Expected: `TotalsMismatch` on the total, computed `0.03`, and nothing recorded. Send it again with a new `requestId` and total `0.03`. Expected: `recorded`, and `get_purchase` shows total `0.03` and each amount exactly as sent.
- **H2, Reformatted receipt identity.** Record Coto. Send Coto again with a new `requestId`, `taxId` `30711883459` (no dashes) and `number` `731562` (no leading zeros). Expected: `already_recorded` with the first purchase's id; `list_purchases` shows one Coto purchase.
- **H3, Partial receipt identity.** Record Farmcity with `receipt` holding `taxId` and `pointOfSale` but no `number`. Expected: `recorded`; when `get_purchase` returns the receipt identity, it holds the printed CUIT and point of sale. Send the same body with a new `requestId`. Expected: `recorded` again, as a second purchase, because a receipt without its full identity is deduplicated only by `requestId`.
- **H4, A retry is answered before its totals.** Record Farmcity without its receipt `number` (so only the `requestId` can find it), with `requestId` R. Send it again with the same R and its total changed to `7195.40`. Expected: `already_recorded` with the stored purchase, whose total is still `7195.50`, and one purchase exists.
- **H5, Ten agents send the same receipt at once.** On an empty database, send Coto ten times concurrently, each with its own `requestId`. Expected: exactly one `recorded` and nine `already_recorded`, all with the same purchase id, and `list_purchases` shows one purchase. A `DatabaseFailure` or any other failure fails the check.
- **H6, Five corrections from one revision at once.** Record Coto (revision 1). Send five `correct_purchase` calls concurrently, each with its own `requestId`, `expectedRevision: 1`, and a different `time`. Expected: exactly one `corrected` and four `RevisionConflict` with `currentRevision: 2`; `get_purchase` shows revision 2 and the winner's time.
- **H7, Money with too many decimals.** Send Farmcity with its first line's amount `4215.0000001`. Then send the original Farmcity with its first line given a quantity of `0.0005` and unit `kg`. Expected: `InvalidInput` both times, and `list_purchases` is empty.
- **H8, A long receipt over a known catalog.** Record Coto. Then record a Coto receipt with a new receipt number and 60 lines: Coto's 27 lines with their codes, then 33 more lines without codes, raw texts `ITEM 28` to `ITEM 60`, each with amount `100.00`; subtotal and total grow by `3300.00` and discounts stay. Expected: `recorded`, `get_purchase` returns 60 lines, and each of lines 1–27 is on the product the same line of the first Coto created, linked by `barcode` or `store_code`.
- **H9, An unknown product writes nothing.** On an empty database, record a receipt at the new chain `Día`, branch `Av. Corrientes 2200`, with one line whose `productId` is `prd_doesnotexist`. Expected: `ProductNotFound`, and `list_stores` does not show Día.
- **H10, A case code is a product identity.** Record a Coto receipt with a new receipt number and one line `GALLO X6` with barcode `27790078024684` (a valid GTIN-14 with packaging indicator 2) and `newProduct`. Then record another such receipt, with another receipt number, the same barcode and a different `newProduct` name. Expected: the second line links to the first line's product, `linkedBy` `barcode`.
- **H11, Same-day purchases without a time (recorded, not scored: the spec leaves this order open, and the owner chose it after the feature shipped).** On an empty database, record two Farmcity receipts dated 2026-11-03 with no `time`, different receipt numbers, the first then the second, a second apart. Expected: `list_purchases` lists the second one first. Repeat with the order of recording swapped, on an empty database: the one recorded last is still first.
- **H12, Paging and the chain filter.** Record Coto, Farmcity, and a second Coto with a new receipt number dated 2026-10-30. Page `list_purchases { limit: 1 }`, following the continuation each answer gives. Expected: three pages, each purchase exactly once, newest first, and the last page offers no continuation. Then `list_purchases { chain: "  coto " }` and `{ chain: "COTO" }` each return the two Coto purchases.
- **H13, MCP and HTTP agree.** Record Coto over HTTP. Over MCP (`/mcp`, protocol revision `2025-06-18`), call `tools/list`, then `get_purchase` for that purchase. Expected: the tool list has `get_status`, `list_stores`, `find_products`, `record_purchase`, `get_purchase`, `list_purchases`, `correct_purchase` and `delete_purchase`, `delete_purchase` with `destructiveHint` and `correct_purchase` with `idempotentHint`; and the purchase in the MCP result (its structured content, or its text content parsed as JSON) equals the HTTP answer's.

## Phase 2: catalog

Run these after phase 2, together with phase 1's checks. Start each check from an empty database and create "A" and "B" by recording two small receipts: A from a Coto line `ARROZ DOBLE CAROLINA` with a fresh valid barcode X and `newProduct` "Arroz doble carolina", no size; B from a Farmcity line `ARROZ CAROLINA X 1KG` without codes and `newProduct` "Arroz carolina 1 kg", no size.

- **H14, Counts are purchases, not lines.** Record a Coto receipt with a new receipt number holding two separate lines with barcode X. Expected: `list_products` shows A with `purchases: 2`, its first receipt and this one, not 3.
- **H15, A correction that keeps a merged line's product.** Merge A into B. Correct A's Coto line keeping its product: send the line complete, with `productId` set to the id `get_purchase` showed for it before the merge (A). Expected: `corrected`, the line shows B. Undo the merge. Expected: the line is back on A with the `linkedBy` it had before the merge.
- **H16, A correction that keeps a line's current product.** Repeat H15, but send `productId` B, the id `get_purchase` shows after the merge. Expected after the undo: the line is back on A with the `linkedBy` it had before the merge.
- **H17, An explicit pick of a merged-away product.** Merge A into B. Correct B's own Farmcity line with `productId` A. Expected: `corrected`, and the line still shows B. Undo the merge. Expected: that Farmcity line is on A with `linkedBy` `chosen`, A's Coto line is on A, and B has no purchases.
- **H18, A correction's new product keeps its kind.** Correct A's Coto line with `newProduct` "Arroz carolina Gallo 1 kg" and `kind` "arroz carolina", keeping barcode X. Expected: the line is on a new product with kind "arroz carolina", and `list_kinds` shows that kind with 1 product.
- **H19, Refused edits create no kind.** Call `update_product { productId: "prd_doesnotexist", kind: "fantasma" }`, then merge A into B and call `update_product { productId: A, kind: "fantasma" }`. Expected: `UnknownProduct`, then `ProductAlreadyMerged`, and `list_kinds` has no "fantasma".
- **H20, Five copies of one merge at once.** Send `merge_products { requestId: M, productId: A, intoProductId: B }` five times concurrently. Expected: exactly one `merged` and four `already_applied`, all with the same merge id, and `list_products { productId: A }` returns B with one entry in `merged[]`.
- **H21, Opposite merges at once.** Send `merge_products` A into B and B into A concurrently, with different `requestId`s. Expected: one `merged` and one `ProductAlreadyMerged`; `list_products` shows exactly one current product of the two.
- **H22, Search finds merged names.** Record a third product C from a Coto line `FIDEOS MONO` with its own barcode and `newProduct` "Fideos moño". Merge A into B. Expected: `list_products { search: "doble" }` returns B and not C, because A, merged into B, has that word in its name.
- **H23, Different units are different sizes.** Give A size 1000 g and B size 1000 ml with `update_product`, then merge A into B. Expected: `SizesDiffer` naming both sizes, and `list_products` still shows A and B as current products, neither with an entry in `merged[]`.
- **H24, Recording after a merge, then deleting it.** Merge A into B. Record a Coto receipt with a new receipt number and a line with barcode X: it links to B. Delete that purchase, then undo the merge. Expected: the undo answers `undone`, A has its original Coto line and B its Farmcity line, `list_purchases` no longer shows the deleted purchase, and `get_purchase` of each remaining purchase shows its original totals.
