# Compare prices in the app, on the phone and the computer

Status: Accepted (by the owner, 2026-11-03)
Issue: https://github.com/taecontrol/despensa/issues/20 (part 3 of #9; #18 and #19 are merged). Owner decisions: this thread, 2026-11-03 (recorded below).

## Problem

- The household wants to know where a product or kind is cheapest and whether a deal was really a deal, in ARS and USD. The two moments are in the store aisle with a phone and at home on the computer (`reported`, #9).
- `compare_prices` answers that on `main` (`d4bba84`), but only an agent can call it. The app has no screen for it, and the README says "comparing prices [is] only available through an agent" (`observed`, `README.md` at `7c53585`).
- Today the owner has to ask an agent to see a comparison (`assumed`).

Options:
- do nothing, so prices stay agent-only;
- a section inside Catálogo or Reposición;
- one Precios screen reading the existing operations (chosen; the owner chose direction A among three prototypes).

## Outcome

On the phone and the computer, a member opens **Precios**, picks a kind or a product, and reads:
- where it was cheapest per kilo, liter or unit, chain by chain;
- which purchases were the best deals against the median;
- every purchase behind one disclosure.

They read it in USD or ARS with one toggle. Every figure is the one `compare_prices` returns.

## Out of scope

- Any new operation, schema change or migration; the screen reads `compare_prices`, `list_kinds` and `list_products` as they are on `main`.
- Charts or price trends over time (direction A has none; the owner chose A).
- Naming the rate source in the UI (owner: no "ArgentinaDatos").
- Links into Precios from other screens (purchase detail, Reposición, Catálogo). This can come later.
- Editing anything from Precios.

## Examples

It was generated by `.work/20-compare-prices-app/seed-generator.sh`, which records them with `record_purchase` and loads MEP rates from `tests/mep-source.ts` through the scheduled sync. It covers:
- kind arroz: 5 products, 4 chains, 12 comparable purchases from 4 jul to 3 nov 2026, plus one line of "Arroz yamaní suelto", which has no size and is listed apart; the purchase on 3 nov has no rate;
- jabón: kg and l;
- leche: l;
- desodorante: without size only;
- Queso cremoso La Paulina: no kind, weighed;
- Next rápida acción 20 comprimidos: per unit;
- yerba: no purchases between 15 oct and 3 nov 2026.

The reference for layout and copy is the accepted prototype at `.work/20-compare-prices-app/prototype/`, with its README and `evidence/`.

- **E15** `capture` **Loading and failures.**
  - While loading, skeletons show in the answer's shape.
  - A network failure shows the Reposición pattern: an Alert with `error.offline` and "Reintentar", which reads again.

- **E16** `capture` **README.** "Using the app" lists five screens, including Precios in the same style as the others. The sentence about agent-only features no longer includes comparing prices.
- **E17** `capture` **Never:**

  - a label contradicts the USD order in ARS mode;
  - a rate source is named;
  - a browser-native control appears (`<select>`, unstyled date/number inputs, `<dialog>`, `alert()`, a loose `<button>`);
  - a layout overflows horizontally at 390 px.

## Decisions

**Owner (2026-11-03, this thread):**
- Direction A, "Dónde conviene": the ranking first, then best deals, then every purchase behind one disclosure. It uses B's subject header with "Cambiar", which opens the picker in place at every width, and B's ARS/USD ToggleGroup. The copy is unslopped and the design is polished with Refactoring UI. The reference is `.work/20-compare-prices-app/prototype/` (README, `evidence/`, `proto-src/`).
- Each row shows only the selected currency. Both currencies appear once per group, on the median line.
- ARS mode keeps the USD order. It labels the order "Más barata en US$" and adds a caption saying the order follows the USD median.
- "Precios" is a navigation destination, last, after Catálogo.
- The UI names no rate source.
- shadcn/ui only, responsive at 390 px and desktop (standing rule).

**Accepted earlier (#19):** every figure, order and rule comes from `compare_prices` as returned.

**Technical (mine, reversible):**
- **Currency:** USD is the default. The choice persists across subjects in the URL. The toggle is disabled when no figure has a currency: an empty period, without-size only, loading or failure.
- **Chain options:** they come from an unfiltered `compare_prices` for the same subject and period.
- **Changing the subject:** it clears the chain, because each subject has its own chains and a kept chain could hide every purchase. It keeps the period and the currency.
- **Phone filter labels:** they are short ("Historial", "Todas", a period like "15 oct a 3 nov"), so the period, the chain and the currency fit one row at 390 px. The full labels show from 1024 px.
- **Picker:** kinds come from `list_kinds`, filtered locally. Products come from `list_products` with `search` and `limit` 20. Products and kinds with no purchases stay selectable and lead to the empty state.

**One-way doors:** none. There is no schema change, no write contract and no new operation. The `/precios` route and its URL parameters are ordinary web state and can change later.

**Program design:** [spec.program-design.md](spec.program-design.md) (Accepted).

**Constraints from the orchestrator and other threads:**
- **App shell:** in `src/web/shell/app-shell.tsx`, append Precios after Catálogo. Do not reorder or remove anything, and keep `<Toaster />`. Keep the change small.
- **shadcn components:** reuse sonner, next-themes, checkbox and alert-dialog as they are on main. Add only `select` and `tabs` with the shadcn CLI. `toggle-group`, `command`, `popover`, `calendar`, `collapsible`, `table`, `badge`, `empty`, `alert` and `skeleton` already exist.
- **README:** the same PR updates its screens section (E16).

## Rabbit holes

- **Rounding and order:** do not format a figure into a rounding other than two decimals, and do not sort for display. `Intl.NumberFormat("es-AR", { style: "currency" })` with `ARS`/`USD` gives `$ 1.598,69` and `US$ 1,02`.
- **Chain filter:** a chain chosen in the Select can drop out of the period's options. Keep it listed while it is selected, so the Select never shows a blank value.
- **Prototype limits:** the prototype disables every picker item, chain and period that has no prepared scenario, including products that do have purchases (such as Gallo Oro). The real screen enables everything, because the operation answers any subject, chain and period.
- **Prototype code:** the prototype's code is a reference for layout and copy, not production code. Rebuild it on the app's patterns: `readQuery`, `failureMessage`, `t()` and route `validateSearch`.
- **Calendar dates:** the calendar's dates are local calendar days (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Never shift them through UTC.
